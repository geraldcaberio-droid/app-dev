# My Favorite Series: Violet Evergarden

## About the Series
*Violet Evergarden* is my favorite anime series. It tells the story of **Violet Evergarden**, a former soldier who learns how to understand human emotions and express them through writing letters.

## Why I Like It
- Beautiful animation
- Emotional and touching story
- Memorable characters
- Inspiring life lessons

### My Favorite Character
> Violet Evergarden is a strong and determined character who learns the true meaning of love and friendship.

### Favorite Quote
> "I want to know what 'I love you' means."

## Main Themes
1. Love
2. Healing
3. Self-discovery
4. Communication

### Fun Facts
- 🎬 Genre: Drama, Fantasy, Slice of Life
- 📺 Episodes: 13
- ⭐ Studio: Kyoto Animation
